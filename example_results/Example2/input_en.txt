Level differences not exceeding 550 mm that could cause falls shall be made perceptible by visual and tactile differentiation. The differentiation shall be at least 250 mm from the edge. The project contains no level differences greater than 55 cm without protective barriers. Protective barriers shall be at least 900 mm high where the difference in level they protect does not exceed 6 m, and 1,100 mm high in all other cases. In this project, all façade protective barriers are standardised at 1,100 mm to unify the design, with sufficient strength and rigidity to withstand the horizontal force established in Section 3.2 of Basic Document SE-AE according to their location. Height shall be measured vertically from floor level or, for stairways, from the pitch line defined by the step vertices to the top edge of the barrier. On the stairway, the guardrail is designed so that its openings are limited to 100 mm. As the stairway is wider than 120 cm, two handrails are provided on each side.
