LLM response

1. Summary of the compliance assessment:
The project generally complies with the regulatory requirements concerning fall protection, barrier height and strength, visual and tactile differentiation of level differences, and the characteristics of stairways and handrails. However, the correct application of visual and tactile differentiation in relation to the minimum distance from the edge must be verified, and it must be confirmed that the guardrail openings strictly comply with the 100 mm limit.

2. Compliance analysis:
2.1 Compliant sections:
- **Visual and tactile differentiation of level differences ≤ 550 mm:** The project states that this differentiation will be provided for level differences not exceeding 550 mm, while the regulations require visual and tactile differentiation for level differences of no more than 55 cm (550 mm) that could cause falls, beginning at least 25 cm from the edge (250 mm in the project). The distance of 250 mm meets the regulatory minimum of 25 cm and is therefore compliant.
- **Height of protective barriers:** The regulations require 0.90 m for level differences ≤ 6 m and 1.10 m in all other cases. The project standardises all façade barriers at 1.10 m, which meets and exceeds the minimum requirement.
- **Measurement of barrier height:** Height is measured vertically from floor level or from the pitch line on stairways, in accordance with the regulations.
- **Barrier strength and rigidity:** The barriers are stated to have sufficient strength in accordance with Section 3.2 of DB SE-AE, which complies with the regulations.
- **Openings in the stair guardrail:** The project limits openings to 100 mm, and the regulations state that they shall not permit the passage of a sphere 10 cm (100 mm) in diameter; 100 mm is therefore compliant.
- **Handrails on stairways wider than 120 cm:** The project provides two handrails on each side, complying with the requirement for handrails on both sides of stairways wider than 1.20 m.
- **Handrail height and characteristics:** Although the query does not give numerical values, the regulations require a height between 90 and 110 cm, a sturdy and easy-to-grip design, at least 4 cm clearance from the wall, and a 30 cm extension at the ends. The project states that the guardrail is suitably formed, with no apparent contradiction.

2.2 Potentially non-compliant sections:
- **Visual and tactile differentiation—distance from the edge:** The regulations state that differentiation shall begin at least 25 cm (250 mm) from the edge, and the project states 'at a distance of at least 250 mm from the edge'. Although numerically compliant, the wording could create uncertainty as to whether it means exactly 250 mm or more. It is recommended to confirm that the differentiation begins at least 250 mm from the edge and never closer.
- **Guardrail openings:** The regulations prohibit openings through which a sphere 10 cm in diameter can pass, and the project sets a limit of 100 mm. This is compliant if measured exactly, but if construction tolerances allow an opening to exceed 100 mm slightly, it may be non-compliant. Precise on-site measurements are recommended.
- **Terminology 'guardrail' versus 'protective barrier':** The regulations distinguish between protective barriers and handrails, whereas the project uses 'guardrail' for the stairway. They are not stated to be equivalent, so it must be ensured that the guardrail meets the characteristics of a protective barrier or handrail, as applicable.

3. Recommendations:
- Confirm that the visual and tactile differentiation actually begins 250 mm or more from the edge, with no area where the distance is shorter.
- Take precise measurements of the guardrail openings to ensure none exceeds 100 mm, taking construction tolerances into account.
- Clarify and verify that the stair 'guardrail' meets the characteristics of a protective barrier or handrail under the regulations, avoiding terminological ambiguity.
- Document the exact handrail height and its clearance from the wall to ensure compliance with the 90–110 cm range and the minimum 4 cm clearance.
- Maintain barrier strength and rigidity in accordance with the regulations and document the tests or calculations demonstrating compliance.
