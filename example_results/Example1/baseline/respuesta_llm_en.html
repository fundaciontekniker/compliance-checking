LLM response

1. Summary of the compliance assessment:
The project includes a stair guardrail 0.95 m high and two handrails at 95 cm and 70 cm (±5 cm), with a design that prevents climbing. The handrail heights are verified as complying with the accessibility ranges, and the design meets the requirement for two handrails in schools or primary education centres. However, the regulations do not define equivalence between a guardrail and a handrail, so each element must be considered separately. No information is available regarding other regulatory requirements for the guardrail (such as fall protection or clearance from the wall).

2. Compliance analysis:
2.1 Compliant sections:
- Upper handrail height of 95 cm: compliant, as it falls within the required 90–110 cm range in Section SUA 1, point 4.
- Lower handrail height of 70 cm (±5 cm): compliant, as 70 cm ±5 cm gives a range of 65–75 cm, which matches the required range for the second handrail in nursery schools and primary education centres (65–75 cm).
- Design preventing the guardrail from being climbable: compliant with the general safety requirement to prevent fall hazards (although this is not specified in detail in the regulations provided, it is an implicit good practice).

2.2 Potentially non-compliant sections:
- Guardrail height of 0.95 m: the regulations do not explicitly establish the required height for stair guardrails, only for handrails. Compliance or non-compliance therefore cannot be confirmed.
- It is not stated whether the guardrail meets the minimum clearance from the wall (at least 4 cm for handrails) or whether the fixing system permits the continuous passage of the hand, requirements applicable to handrails under Section SUA 1, point 5.
- It is not stated whether the stairway is wider than 1.20 m, which is needed to verify whether handrails are mandatory on both sides and whether intermediate handrails may be required.
- No handrail extension at the ends (30 cm) is mentioned, as required by Section SUA 1, point 3 for public-use or healthcare stairways.

3. Recommendations:
- Verify and document the specific height and characteristics of the guardrail, since the regulations do not define clear requirements for stair guardrails; it is recommended that the guardrail meet fall-protection criteria (minimum height, strength, etc.).
- Confirm that the handrails have at least 4 cm clearance from the wall and that their fixing system does not obstruct the continuous passage of the hand.
- Confirm whether the stairway exceeds 1.20 m in width to ensure that handrails are installed on both sides and, if it exceeds 2.40 m, that intermediate handrails are provided.
- Ensure that the handrails extend 30 cm beyond the ends on at least one side if the stairway is for public or healthcare use.
- Document compliance with other regulatory requirements applicable to stairways (clear headroom, risers, lighting, tactile signage, etc.) to ensure overall compliance.
