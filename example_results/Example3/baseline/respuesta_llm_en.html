LLM response

1. Summary of the compliance assessment:
Overall, the project complies with the regulatory requirements for general-use stairways regarding tread and riser dimensions, the relationship between them, flight width, landings and clear-passage conditions. The storey and basement heights do not contravene the regulations provided. The definition and measurement of clear width and the handrail condition are consistent with the requirements. No explicit non-compliance is identified for ramps, provided that the gradient does not exceed 4%, in which case the route would not be regarded as a ramp.

2. Compliance analysis:
2.1 Compliant sections:
- **Height between storeys:** The height of 3.00 m between storeys and 4.00 m on the ground floor, together with the variable basement heights (2.90 to 4.68 m), does not contravene any regulatory restriction on maximum or minimum height between storeys.
- **Tread–riser relationship:** The relationship stated in the project is 540 mm ≤ 2R + T ≤ 700 mm, corresponding to 54 cm ≤ 2R + T ≤ 70 cm in the regulations, and is therefore compliant.
- **Tread and riser dimensions:** A minimum tread of 28 cm and risers between 13 and 17.5 cm comply with the regulations, which establish a minimum tread of 28 cm and risers between 13 cm and 18.5 cm (or 17.5 cm in areas without a lift).
- **Flight width:** 130 cm exceeds the required minimum (Table 4.1 is not included, but 1.20 m is the reference threshold for handrails on both sides), and is therefore compliant.
- **Clear width and measurement:** The clear width is measured between walls or barriers without deducting handrails that project no more than 12 cm, in accordance with the regulations.
- **Landings:** Landings with the same dimension as the flight and a clear length greater than 1 m, free of obstacles and not encroached upon by door swings, comply with points 4.2.3.1 and 4.2.3.2.
- **Ramps:** A route is considered a ramp if its gradient exceeds 4%, and the entrance-lobby floor is at the same level as the covered entrance, which does not contradict the regulations.

2.2 Potentially non-compliant sections:
- The query does not state whether the steps have vertical risers or risers inclined at an angle ≤15°, as required to avoid nosings (4.2.1.2).
- Uniformity of tread and riser dimensions between flights and consecutive storeys is not mentioned (4.2.1.3).
- It is not stated whether protective barriers meet the minimum-height requirement (0.90 m or 1.10 m, depending on the case) or whether they are designed to prevent children from climbing them (3.2.3.1).
- The existence and characteristics of handrails are not mentioned. Although the width is stated as 130 cm and it is indicated that width is measured without deducting handrails projecting no more than 12 cm, it is not confirmed that handrails are provided on one or both sides according to the height difference overcome (4.2.4.1).
- It is not expressly stated whether a landing at a change of direction maintains the full width without reduction (4.2.3.2). Although it is said to have the same dimension as the rest of the stairway, this should be confirmed.
- It is not stated whether, on curved flights, the tread complies with the measurement 50 cm from the inner edge and the maximum of 44 cm at the outer edge (4.2.1.3), although no curved flights are indicated.

3. Recommendations:
- Confirm that risers are vertical or inclined at an angle ≤15° to avoid nosings.
- Verify that tread and riser dimensions are uniform on consecutive flights and storeys, and that the variation in riser height between consecutive flights does not exceed 1 cm.
- Ensure that protective barriers meet the required minimum height and the characteristics intended to prevent children from climbing them.
- Confirm the installation of handrails on one or both sides according to the height difference overcome and flight width, and that they project no more than 12 cm.
- Verify that the landing at a change of direction maintains the full width without reduction, is free of obstacles and is not obstructed by doors.
- For curved flights, check that the tread meets the specific dimensions 50 cm from the inner and outer edges.
- For ramps, confirm that the gradient does not exceed 4% if the route is not to be considered a ramp; otherwise, comply with the specific ramp regulations.
