LLM response

1. Summary of the compliance assessment:
- The project describes stairway features that generally appear to align with the regulatory requirements for general-use stairways, particularly in relation to tread and riser dimensions, width and landings. However, no information is provided on several key aspects, such as the strength and characteristics of barriers and handrails or the type of flights (straight, curved or mixed), creating uncertainty as to full compliance. There is no explicit regulatory requirement against which to validate storey and basement heights, so they are not assessed. For ramps, the information is insufficient to verify regulatory compliance.

2. Compliance analysis:

2.1 Compliant sections:
- Step dimensional relationship: The relationship 54 cm ≤ 2R + T ≤ 70 cm, with a minimum tread of 28 cm and risers between 13 and 17.5 cm, complies with Section SUA 1, 4 Stairways and ramps, 4.2 General-use stairways, points 3 and 4 (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.2 General-use stairways).
- Flight width: A clear width of 130 cm, measured between walls or barriers without deducting handrails that project no more than 12 cm, complies with point 5 of the same section (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.2 General-use stairways).
- Landings: The landing at flights with a change of direction has the same dimension as the rest of the stairway, is more than 100 cm and free of obstacles, and is not encroached upon by doors. It therefore complies with the requirements of Section SUA 1, 4.2.3 Landings, points 1 and 2 (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.2 General-use stairways).
- Clear width free of obstacles and regulatory measurement: It is stated that the width is measured between walls or barriers without deducting handrails that project no more than 12 cm, in accordance with point 5 (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.2 General-use stairways).

2.2 Potentially non-compliant sections or areas lacking evidence:
- Type of flights (straight, curved or mixed): It is not specified whether the flights are straight, curved or mixed, or whether the restriction for specific areas (hospital wards, nursery schools and primary/secondary schools) requiring straight flights is met (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.2 General-use stairways, point 2).
- Uniformity of tread and riser dimensions between consecutive flights: It is not stated whether the riser variation between consecutive flights is less than 1 cm or whether the tread is uniform on straight flights (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.2 General-use stairways, point 3).
- Absence of nosings: It is not stated whether the steps are free of nosings, as required for general-use stairways (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.2 General-use stairways, point 4).
- Risers: It is not stated whether the risers are vertical or inclined by no more than 15° on stairways used for upward evacuation or where there is no alternative accessible route (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.2 General-use stairways, point 5).
- Tread measurement on curved flights: It is not specified whether the minimum tread of 28 cm is achieved 50 cm from the inner edge on curved flights or whether the maximum at the outer edge is 44 cm (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.2 General-use stairways, point 6).
- Minimum number of steps per flight: It is not stated whether each flight has at least 3 steps (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.2 General-use stairways, point 7).
- Maximum height difference overcome per flight: The maximum height difference overcome by each flight is not stated (225 cm in public areas or where there is no lift, and 320 cm in other cases) (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.2 General-use stairways, point 8).
- Handrails: No information is provided on the existence, height, continuity, extension, firmness, distance from the wall or other characteristics of the handrails (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.2 General-use stairways, 4.2.4 Handrails).
- Protective barriers: The height, strength, design to prevent climbing by children and maximum permitted openings in the barriers are not mentioned (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 3 Level differences > 3.2 Characteristics of protective barriers).
- On curved flights, exclusion of areas with tread depths of less than 17 cm when measuring clear width: It is not stated whether this exclusion is applied (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.2 General-use stairways, point 5).
- Ramps: The information on ramps is insufficient to assess compliance with maximum gradients, width, landings, handrails, kerbs and slip-resistant flooring (SUA 1. Safety from the risk of falls > 4 Stairways and ramps > 4.3 Ramps).
- Storey and basement heights: There are no explicit regulatory requirements against which to validate storey or basement heights, so they are not assessed (not assessable due to the absence of a regulatory reference).

3. Recommendations:
- Specify the type of flights (straight, curved or mixed) and verify compliance with the restrictions for specific areas.
- Confirm the uniformity of tread and riser dimensions between consecutive flights, with a maximum riser variation of 1 cm.
- Ensure that steps have no nosings.
- Detail the characteristics of the risers, particularly on stairways used for upward evacuation or where no alternative accessible route exists.
- For curved flights, verify tread measurement in accordance with the regulations and exclude areas with tread depths of less than 17 cm from the clear-width measurement.
- Confirm that each flight has at least 3 steps and that the maximum height difference overcome per flight is within the regulatory limits.
- Include information on handrails: number, height, continuity, extension, firmness and distance from the wall.
- Detail the characteristics of protective barriers: height, strength, anti-climb design and maximum permitted openings.
- Provide complete information on ramps, including gradient, width, landings, handrails, kerbs and slip-resistant flooring.
- Storey and basement heights do not need to be validated against the regulations provided.

This analysis is based exclusively on the regulations and information provided, without assuming any conditions that have not been explicitly stated.
