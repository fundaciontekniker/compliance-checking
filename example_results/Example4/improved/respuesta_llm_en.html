LLM response

1. Summary of the compliance assessment:
- The project partially complies with the accessibility regulations for dwellings, particularly regarding doors and circulation spaces. However, discrepancies have been identified in the minimum clear-passage dimensions of internal doors and the minimum corridor width, which do not reach the regulatory values. In addition, there is no evidence of compliance with specific requirements for peepholes in entrance doors, the opening direction of toilet doors, or details of furniture and minimum clear spaces in rooms, creating a risk of regulatory non-compliance.

2. Compliance analysis:

2.1 Compliant sections:
- Dwelling entrance door with a minimum clear passage of 90 cm: complies with the minimum clear-opening dimension of 90 cm required for dwelling entrance doors (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.2.1).
- Minimum clear height of 200 cm for entrance doors and internal rooms: the regulations do not specify an exact minimum door height, but this dimension is not contradicted and is therefore considered compliant in the absence of a contrary reference (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.1).
- Terraces accessible from inside without steps or steep gradients: this complies with the requirement for terraces without steep gradients and with slip-resistant grilles to prevent tripping (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.1).
- Circles 120 cm in diameter in the entrance hall and in front of doors to rooms: although the regulations for adapted dwellings require circles of 180 cm, they do not prohibit smaller circles in other cases, so this is considered partially compliant (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.2.6).
- Doors operable by people with manipulation difficulties: the regulations state that doors shall be capable of being opened and operated by people with manipulation difficulties; this is assumed to be met unless otherwise indicated (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.2.1).

2.2 Potentially non-compliant sections:
- Doors providing access to internal rooms with a minimum clear passage width of 80 cm: the regulations establish a minimum clear-opening dimension of 80 cm for internal doors, but also state that the corridor shall be at least 95 cm wide at every point (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.1). The project states that circulation spaces are at least 1.00 m wide but does not specifically state that corridors are at least 95 cm wide, creating uncertainty.
- Entrance hall with a circle 120 cm in diameter: the regulations for adapted dwellings require an obstacle-free turning space 180 cm in diameter, including clear space on both sides of the entrance door (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.2.1). The project specifies 120 cm, which is insufficient and does not comply with the requirement for adapted dwellings.
- A circle 120 cm in diameter in front of doors providing access to rooms: the regulations for rooms (bedrooms and living-dining rooms) require circles 180 cm in diameter to permit turning and accessibility (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.2.6). The project specifies 120 cm, which does not comply with the regulations for adapted dwellings.
- Toilet doors: the query does not state the direction in which they open. They must never open inwards and may be sliding doors (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.2.1). Evidence is lacking.
- Dwelling entrance doors with two peepholes, the lower one below 110 cm: this is not mentioned in the query, so evidence is lacking to verify compliance (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.2.1).
- Corridors at least 95 cm wide: the query states that circulation spaces are at least 1.00 m wide but does not specify that corridors are at least 95 cm wide at every point, which may be an omission or lack of detail (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.1).
- Furniture and clear spaces in rooms: the regulations require clear passages 90 cm wide and circles 180 cm in diameter in bedrooms and living rooms for wheelchair users (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.2.6). The query specifies passages of 80 cm and circles of 120 cm, which do not comply with the regulations for adapted dwellings.
- Operable doors and knobs/handles: the query does not state whether they comply with the maximum projection of 7 cm or whether colour contrast is provided (ANNEX III > Article 5 > 5.2.2). Evidence is lacking.
- Toilet doors that never open inwards: this is not mentioned in the query. Evidence is lacking (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.2.1).
- Clear turning space 180 cm in diameter in the entrance hall and in front of entrance doors: the query specifies 120 cm, failing to meet the requirement for adapted dwellings (ANNEX III > Article 10 > 10.2.2.1 and 10.2.2.6).
- Minimum clear height beneath stairways and other conditions for internal stairways: this is not mentioned in the query. Evidence is lacking (ANNEX III > Article 5 > 5.3.1).

3. Recommendations:
- Adjust the turning-circle diameter in the entrance hall and in front of doors to rooms to 180 cm in order to comply with the regulations for adapted dwellings.
- Ensure that internal doors have a minimum clear opening of 90 cm, particularly in adapted dwellings, and that corridors are at least 95 cm wide at every point.
- Provide two peepholes in dwelling entrance doors, with the lower one 110 cm above the floor, and an obstacle-free turning space 180 cm in diameter on both sides of the door.
- Ensure that toilet doors open outwards or are sliding doors, in accordance with the regulations.
- Verify that knobs and handles project no more than 7 cm and that adequate colour contrast is provided between doors and walls.
- Review and document compliance with the door-operability and room-clearance conditions, adjusting clear passage widths to 90 cm and turning circles to 180 cm where required.
- Include details in the project on internal stairways, the clear height beneath them and handrails in accordance with the regulations.
- Complete the documentation with evidence of compliance with all the stated regulatory requirements for adapted dwellings, in order to avoid risks of non-compliance.
