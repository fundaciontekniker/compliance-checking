ANNEX III. TECHNICAL CONDITIONS FOR ACCESSIBILITY IN BUILDINGS > Article 10.– RESIDENTIAL BUILDINGS. > 10.2.– CONDITIONS INSIDE DWELLINGS. > 10.2.2.– DWELLINGS FOR WHEELCHAIR USERS OR PEOPLE WITH REDUCED MOBILITY. > 10.2.2.1.– Doors and clear openings.

Source: results/anexo_decreto_68_2000/anexo_decreto_68_2000_full.json

1.– The entrance door to the dwelling shall have two peepholes, the lower one positioned 110 cm above the floor. On both sides there shall be a horizontal clear space, outside the sweep of the door leaves, allowing an obstacle-free turning circle 180 cm in diameter. 2.– Entrance doors and internal doors of the dwelling shall have a minimum clear-opening dimension of 90 cm. 3.– Doors shall be capable of being opened and operated by people with manipulation difficulties. 4.– Toilet doors shall never open inwards and may be sliding doors.

ANNEX III. TECHNICAL CONDITIONS FOR ACCESSIBILITY IN BUILDINGS > Article 10.– RESIDENTIAL BUILDINGS. > 10.2.– CONDITIONS INSIDE DWELLINGS. > 10.2.2.– DWELLINGS FOR WHEELCHAIR USERS OR PEOPLE WITH REDUCED MOBILITY. > 10.2.2.6.– Rooms.

Source: results/anexo_decreto_68_2000/anexo_decreto_68_2000_full.json

a) Bedrooms 1.– At least one of the bedrooms in the dwelling shall be a double bedroom. 2.– In each bedroom, a circle 180 cm in diameter shall be capable of being inscribed in front of the entrance door. 3.– On both sides of the bed there shall be a clear space at least 90 cm wide, and at the foot of the bed, 110 cm. b) Living rooms 1.– Furniture shall be arranged so that it is accessible via a clear passage 90 cm wide and an obstacle-free space in which a circle 180 cm in diameter can be inscribed. 2.– Where the dining function is incorporated into the aforementioned living rooms or kitchens, their floor area shall be increased.

ANNEX III. TECHNICAL CONDITIONS FOR ACCESSIBILITY IN BUILDINGS > Article 5.– INTERNAL COMMUNICATIONS. > 5.2.– HORIZONTAL COMMUNICATIONS. > 5.2.2.– DOORS.

Source: results/anexo_decreto_68_2000/anexo_decreto_68_2000_full.json

1.– Doors inside the building shall comply with the provisions of point 4.1.1 of this Annex concerning external entrance doors; turning diameters of 120 cm shall be permitted on the side of corridors of that width, provided additionally that knobs and handles project no more than 7 cm from the plane of the door. 2.– Colour contrast between doors and walls should be provided in accordance with the characteristics of Annex IV on Accessibility in Communication. 3.– Where a door has a window or peephole to make the occupancy of a room visible, its design shall take account of use by people of short stature or seated in a wheelchair, either by providing two peepholes, one of them 110 cm above the floor, or a single peephole extending down to that height.

ANNEX III. TECHNICAL CONDITIONS FOR ACCESSIBILITY IN BUILDINGS > Article 10.– RESIDENTIAL BUILDINGS. > 10.2.– CONDITIONS INSIDE DWELLINGS. > 10.2.1.– COMMON CONDITIONS.

Source: results/anexo_decreto_68_2000/anexo_decreto_68_2000_full.json

The following conditions shall be met inside dwellings: – The clear opening or entrance door into the dwelling shall have a minimum width of 90 cm. – The minimum clear opening dimension of doors inside the dwelling shall be 80 cm. – The corridor shall have a minimum width at all points of 95 cm. – In dwellings extending over more than one storey, technical and design provisions shall be made to facilitate the possible installation of a lift. – External window and door units shall be operable, or accessibility for their maintenance and cleaning shall be ensured. – Where the dwelling has a terrace, it shall be constructed without steep gradients and the drains shall be fitted with grilles made of slip-resistant material even when wet. The grilles shall have maximum openings of 1 cm and a grid arrangement that prevents tripping by people using walking sticks or wheelchairs. – Where a door-entry system is installed, it shall be a video door-entry system.

ANNEX III. TECHNICAL ACCESSIBILITY REQUIREMENTS FOR BUILDINGS > Article 5.– INTERNAL COMMUNICATION ROUTES. > 5.3.– VERTICAL COMMUNICATION ROUTES. > 5.3.1.– STAIRWAYS.

Source: results/anexo_decreto_68_2000/anexo_decreto_68_2000_full.json

In addition to complying with the applicable regulations, the design and layout of stairways shall comply with the specifications set out below: – Isolated steps shall not be constructed. – The minimum clear headroom beneath stairways shall be 220 cm. – Stairways shall have risers and shall not have nosings. – Overlapping steps are prohibited. – The soffit of the lowest flight of the stairway shall be enclosed up to a minimum height of 220 cm. – All stairways shall be fitted with handrails on both sides if they exceed 120 cm in width, and along the entire usable route across landings and intermediate landings. The handrails shall have the characteristics indicated in point 5.3.3 below. When the stairway width exceeds 240 cm, intermediate handrails shall also be installed. – Tactile signage shall be provided at the entrances to stairways, with the characteristics established in Annex IV on Accessibility in Communication. – Stairways shall be properly illuminated in accordance with Annex IV on Accessibility in Communication.
