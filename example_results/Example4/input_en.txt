All dwellings are designed to comply with the following:
 
▪ Dwelling entrance door with a minimum clear passage of 0.90 m and a minimum clear height of 2.00 m.
 
▪ Entrance hall in which a circle 1.20 m in diameter can be inscribed without encroaching on the door-swing area.
 
▪ Doors providing access to internal rooms have a minimum clear passage width of 0.80 m and a minimum clear height of 2.00 m. In adapted dwellings, these dimensions are adjusted to the requirements of Decree 68/2000.
 
▪ All circulation spaces have a minimum width of 1.00 m.
 
▪ A circle 1.20 m in diameter can be inscribed in front of the doors providing access to at least one full bathroom, the living-dining-kitchen area and one bedroom. In some cases, the circle is inscribed with the doors open.
 
▪ Internal routes through rooms have a minimum clear passage width of 0.80 m, allowing access to the main item of furniture in each space.
 
▪ Outdoor terraces are accessible from inside without steps.
