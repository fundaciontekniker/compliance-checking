LLM response

1. Summary of the compliance assessment:
Overall, the project complies with the minimum dimensions for doors and circulation spaces and with accessibility to terraces under the regulations provided. However, there are discrepancies in the diameters of turning circles and the minimum widths of corridors and internal spaces, which do not match the more stringent values established for adapted dwellings or wheelchair users.

2. Compliance analysis:
2.1 Compliant sections:
- Dwelling entrance door with a minimum clear passage of 0.90 m and a minimum clear height of 2.00 m: compliant with Articles 10.2.1 and 10.2.2.1, which establish a minimum width of 0.90 m and a minimum clear height (although the regulations do not specify a numerical minimum height, 2.00 m is customary and is not contradicted).
- Doors providing access to internal rooms, with a minimum clear passage width of 0.80 m and a minimum clear height of 2.00 m: compliant with Article 10.2.1, which establishes a minimum of 0.80 m for internal doors.
- Circulation spaces at least 1.00 m wide: partially compliant, since the regulations specify a minimum corridor width of 0.95 m (Article 10.2.1) and 1.20 m in entrance halls and corridors for adapted dwellings (Article 10.2.2.2). Therefore, 1.00 m complies with the common conditions but not with those for adapted dwellings.
- Terraces accessible from inside without steps: compliant with the regulations, which require terraces to be accessible without steep gradients or barriers (Article 10.2.1).

2.2 Potentially non-compliant sections:
- A circle 1.20 m in diameter in the entrance hall and in front of doors giving access to a bathroom, living-dining-kitchen area and bedroom: the regulations for adapted dwellings require an obstacle-free circle 1.80 m in diameter (Articles 10.2.2.6 and 10.2.2.2). Therefore, 1.20 m is less than required for full accessibility.
- Internal routes with a minimum clear passage width of 0.80 m: the regulations establish 0.95 m for corridors in general and 1.20 m in entrance halls and corridors for adapted dwellings (Articles 10.2.1 and 10.2.2.2). Therefore, 0.80 m may be insufficient for full accessibility.
- In adapted dwellings, door and space dimensions must comply with Decree 68/2000, but the information does not state whether that decree is met, so verification remains pending.

3. Recommendations:
- Increase the diameter of the turning circle in the entrance hall and in front of key doors to 1.80 m to comply with the regulations for adapted dwellings and ensure a wheelchair can make a complete turn.
- Increase the minimum width of corridors and internal routes to at least 0.95 m (preferably 1.20 m in entrance halls and distribution areas) to ensure regulatory accessibility.
- Verify and ensure that dimensions in adapted dwellings comply with Decree 68/2000, particularly for doors and circulation spaces.
- Maintain a minimum clear height of 2.00 m at doors and access points. Although this is not contradicted, it is advisable to confirm that no obstacles reduce this height.
