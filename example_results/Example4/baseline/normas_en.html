ANNEX III. TECHNICAL CONDITIONS FOR ACCESSIBILITY IN BUILDINGS > Article 10.– RESIDENTIAL BUILDINGS. > 10.2.– CONDITIONS INSIDE DWELLINGS. > 10.2.2.– DWELLINGS FOR WHEELCHAIR USERS OR PEOPLE WITH REDUCED MOBILITY. > 10.2.2.6.– Rooms.

Source: results/anexo_decreto_68_2000/anexo_decreto_68_2000_full.json

a) Bedrooms 1.– At least one of the bedrooms in the dwelling shall be a double bedroom. 2.– In each bedroom, a circle 1.80 m in diameter shall be capable of being inscribed in front of the entrance door. 3.– On both sides of the bed there shall be a clear space at least 0.90 m wide, and at the foot of the bed, 1.10 m. b) Living rooms 1.– Furniture shall be arranged so that it is accessible via a clear passage 0.90 m wide and an obstacle-free space in which a circle 1.80 m in diameter can be inscribed. 2.– Where the dining function is incorporated into the aforementioned living rooms or kitchens, their floor area shall be increased.

ANNEX III. TECHNICAL CONDITIONS FOR ACCESSIBILITY IN BUILDINGS > Article 10.– RESIDENTIAL BUILDINGS. > 10.2.– CONDITIONS INSIDE DWELLINGS. > 10.2.2.– DWELLINGS FOR WHEELCHAIR USERS OR PEOPLE WITH REDUCED MOBILITY. > 10.2.2.1.– Doors and clear openings.

Source: results/anexo_decreto_68_2000/anexo_decreto_68_2000_full.json

1.– The entrance door to the dwelling shall have two peepholes, the lower one positioned 1.10 m above the floor. On both sides there shall be a horizontal clear space, outside the sweep of the door leaves, allowing an obstacle-free turning circle 1.80 m in diameter. 2.– Entrance doors and internal doors of the dwelling shall have a minimum clear-opening dimension of 90 cm. 3.– Doors shall be capable of being opened and operated by people with manipulation difficulties. 4.– Toilet doors shall never open inwards and may be sliding doors.

ANNEX III. TECHNICAL CONDITIONS FOR ACCESSIBILITY IN BUILDINGS > Article 10.– RESIDENTIAL BUILDINGS. > 10.2.– CONDITIONS INSIDE DWELLINGS. > 10.2.1.– COMMON CONDITIONS.

Source: results/anexo_decreto_68_2000/anexo_decreto_68_2000_full.json

The following conditions shall be met inside dwellings: – The clear opening or entrance door into the dwelling shall have a minimum width of 0.90 m. – The minimum clear opening dimension of doors inside the dwelling shall be 0.80 m. – The corridor shall have a minimum width at all points of 0.95 m. – In dwellings extending over more than one storey, technical and design provisions shall be made to facilitate the possible installation of a lift. – External window and door units shall be operable, or accessibility for their maintenance and cleaning shall be ensured. – Where the dwelling has a terrace, it shall be constructed without steep gradients and the drains shall be fitted with grilles made of slip-resistant material even when wet. The grilles shall have maximum openings of 1 cm and a grid arrangement that prevents tripping by people using walking sticks or wheelchairs. – Where a door-entry system is installed, it shall be a video door-entry system.

ANNEX III. TECHNICAL ACCESSIBILITY REQUIREMENTS FOR BUILDINGS > Article 5.– INTERNAL COMMUNICATION ROUTES. > 5.3.– VERTICAL COMMUNICATION ROUTES. > 5.3.1.– STAIRWAYS.

Source: results/anexo_decreto_68_2000/anexo_decreto_68_2000_full.json

In addition to complying with the applicable regulations, the design and layout of stairways shall comply with the specifications set out below: – Isolated steps shall not be constructed. – The minimum clear headroom beneath stairways shall be 2.20 m. – Stairways shall have risers and shall not have nosings. – Overlapping steps are prohibited. – The soffit of the lowest flight of the stairway shall be enclosed up to a minimum height of 2.20 m. – All stairways shall be fitted with handrails on both sides if they exceed 1.20 m in width, and along the entire usable route across landings and intermediate landings. The handrails shall have the characteristics indicated in Section 5.3.3 below. When the stairway width exceeds 2.40 m, intermediate handrails shall also be installed. – Tactile signage shall be provided at the entrances to stairways, with the characteristics established in Annex IV on Accessibility in Communication. – Stairways shall be properly illuminated in accordance with Annex IV on Accessibility in Communication.
